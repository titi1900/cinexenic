Privacy Policy

How Cinexenic collects, uses, and protects your data — with full transparency, in accordance with the GDPR.

1. Data Controller

The Cinexenic website and waitlist are published as part of the project incubated at EuropaTech (Berlin, Germany). Contact for any question regarding personal data:

Contact: contact@cinexenic.example

2. Data Collected

Via the waitlist form, we collect only: your email address and your area of interest (sports, health, ergonomics, creation). No biometric data, no video content, and no advertising trackers are collected on this website.

The language is chosen by following the FR, EN or DE link: each version has its own address and no preference is stored in your browser.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis

Your data is used exclusively to inform you about the opening of pre-orders and the first Cinexenic software modules. Processing is based on your consent, given via the form checkbox. You may withdraw it at any time.

4. Retention Period

Your data is kept for the duration of the waitlist, then for a maximum of 3 years after the last contact, unless you request deletion or withdraw your consent.

5. Recipients and Processors

Only Cinexenic project members have access to this data. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). Any email delivery services used to contact you operate under data processing agreements compliant with Article 28 of the GDPR.

6. Transfers Outside the European Union

Hosting in the United States by GitHub is governed by the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. No other transfer outside the EU takes place.

7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability of your data, as well as the right to withdraw your consent. To exercise them, write to us; a response will be provided within one month.

You may also lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority of your country of residence.

8. Security and Updates

Communication with the website is encrypted (HTTPS). If this policy is modified, the update date will be revised and, where necessary, an announcement will be published on the home page.
