Technological Breakthrough

From gesture to data, in three steps

  1. Wear

    Put on the ultra-light device. No camera to set up, no video markers, no studio.

  2. Move

    Play, work, train: the embedded sensors continuously record your biomechanics.

  3. Understand

    Visualize the raw data, then the gesture scoring on mobile, tablet or a Cloud dashboard.

Privacy by Design

Measuring without filming is a design choice

A movement is sensitive personal data. We treat privacy as a core product feature.

No images

The device only embeds inertial sensors (IMU): no camera, no risk of visual leakage.

Data Sovereignty

We only collect the metrics essential to your use, with end-to-end encryption.

Total Transparency

Storage, governance and data export rights are clearly described from sign-up onwards.

Faq

Frequently asked questions

What is Cinexenic?

Cinexenic is a wearable device that turns human movement into usable data, without any camera or heavy infrastructure.

Do I need a camera or an equipped room?

No. The device is worn on the body and measures movement directly.

What is it used for?

The first target uses are sports, gesture tracking, RSI prevention and digital creation.

When can I buy it?

Pre-orders will open soon. Join the waiting list.

Is my motion data protected?

The device captures no images. Strict GDPR compliance.
